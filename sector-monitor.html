Method and coverage

Yahoo Finance closes are adjusted for dividends and splits. Each current S&P 500 issuer has one vote, using its alphabetically first listed Yahoo symbol. The market row averages the covered issuers over the same dates.

Broad leaders beat the three-month market average and have a majority of members ahead. Improving laggards trailed over three months, led over 20 sessions, and had more members rise than in the preceding 20 sessions, measured on the same companies. Narrow rallies have an above-market average and an at-or-below-market median.

Thin groups have fewer than five covered companies or less than 80% coverage. They are excluded from the shortlists and extremes.
