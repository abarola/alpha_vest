Methodology

01

Collect

The deployment scripts retrieve public, IPO, or exited companies from each fund’s published portfolio source.

02

Normalize

Company names are cleaned and matched with a normalized key so spelling and punctuation differences do not split the same name.

03

Compare

Each company receives one count per portfolio. A higher count means more of the tracked funds report the same company.

This is a portfolio-overlap screen, not a performance ranking or investment recommendation. Source definitions differ by fund and may include public companies, IPOs, acquisitions, or selected exits.
